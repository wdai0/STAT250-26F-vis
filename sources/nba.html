Methods and data

Source: supplied nba23.csv. Counts and minutes are totals, not per-game values; no season is inferred from the filename. Salary currency and the FP definition are unverified.

Quartiles: linear interpolation (Type 7). Whiskers: extreme observed values within 1.5 × IQR of Q1 and Q3. Sample SD uses n − 1 and is undefined for n < 2. Missing values are excluded by variable and counted after filtering. All groups share the full-data scale; empty groups keep their positions.
