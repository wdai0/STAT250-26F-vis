These 52 flashcards cover 13 topics from STAT 250 Modules 3–4. Numerical scenarios are illustrative teaching examples.

Sampling-distribution examples use independent draws with replacement from values 0, 2, and 10, with probabilities 0.50, 0.30, and 0.20; μ = 2.6 and σ = 3.8. Simulation plots are reproducible. The bootstrap example resamples the five records 2, 4, 4, 6, 9. Percentiles use linear interpolation. Null curves are explicitly illustrative Normal models; they do not add formal z- or t-test calculations to the review.

Decision cards use the course convention: reject when p-value ≤ α. Interval/test examples use matched two-sided procedures and avoid equality at endpoints.
